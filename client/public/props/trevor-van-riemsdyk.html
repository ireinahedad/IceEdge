Why the sample is so small

Pittsburgh signed van Riemsdyk on July 1 to a two year deal worth 4 million a season, after six seasons in Washington, to fix a defense that gave up too many chances. He was hurt against Columbus in October, went on injured reserve, and rejoined line rushes at practice on November 13, paired with Ilya Solovyov.

A veteran defensive defenseman coming back from injury usually plays limited minutes at first, with penalty kill duty, and that keeps his shots and points lines low. The rates in the table below come from only a handful of games and should be read that way.
