Kiviranta back in Dallas

Kiviranta returned to Dallas on a one year, 1 million dollar deal in July, after three seasons in Colorado. His last season there was broken up by injuries, with nine points in 51 games, though he won Olympic bronze with Finland in February.

Stars fans remember his Game 7 hat trick in 2020, but his props are about role, not memory. He has not dressed for every Dallas game this season, so the first check on any night is whether he plays at all. When he does, low ice time keeps his shots line near the minimum.
