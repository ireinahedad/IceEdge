What is moving Makar's props right now

Makar left the November 12 game in Ottawa with a lower body injury, the same night Nathan MacKinnon was hurt, and Colorado put his recovery at about two weeks. Until he is back there is no Makar prop to price, and the first games after his return deserve caution: teams often ease a returning defenseman back in, and his shot and point rates live on his minutes.

Healthy, he runs the Avalanche power play and is one of the few defensemen whose points line is set like a forward's. MacKinnon is expected to be out longer than Makar, which cuts both ways: more of the offense has to run through the blue line, but the forward he most often sets up is missing. His role is not in question, since Colorado signed him to an eight year extension that runs through 2035. Only his health is.
