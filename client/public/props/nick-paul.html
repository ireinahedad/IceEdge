Paul in Toronto

Toronto acquired Paul from Tampa Bay on July 1 for goalie Dennis Hildeby and two draft picks, bringing a Mississauga native home after years of hurting the Leafs, including both Tampa goals in Game 7 of the 2022 first round. He came in with a healthy wrist after an injury hit season of 15 points in 51 games.

His usage is why his props are worth a look. The Hockey News reported he has skated with Kirill Marchenko and William Nylander, and he plays on Toronto's top penalty kill unit. A grinder on a skilled line usually gets more shots than his scoring history suggests, while penalty kill time does little for a points line. Of his props, shots is the more natural one.
