Borgen's minutes in Boston

Boston traded a 2027 second round pick and a conditional 2028 third to the Rangers for Borgen on July 1, to fix the right side of its defense. He has settled onto a second pair with Nikita Zadorov that the Boston Globe called an unexpected shutdown pairing, and through 18 games he ranked third on the team in total ice time.

He is not a scorer: five goals last season matched his career high. That makes his points line a long shot and his shots line a function of minutes. More ice time than he had in New York lifts his shot volume a little without changing what kind of player he is.
