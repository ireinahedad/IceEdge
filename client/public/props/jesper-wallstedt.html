Why Wallstedt's saves line exists every night

Wallstedt took the Minnesota net in last spring's playoffs and kept it. With Filip Gustavsson out after offseason hip surgery, the Wild started him in every game to open the season. For a saves prop that matters more than his save percentage: the number of starts decides how often the line is posted at all.

Watch the crease as Gustavsson returns. A tandem turns a nightly saves bet into a question of who starts, and confirmed starters often arrive only hours before puck drop. Wallstedt is also in the final year of his two year, 2.2 million dollar contract.
