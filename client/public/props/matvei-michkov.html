Goals, minutes and the trade noise

Michkov leads the Flyers in goals, yet a November usage check found him sixth among Philadelphia forwards in average ice time. That gap is the prop story: a winger finishing at this rate on fewer minutes than a top line usually gets. If his minutes rise, his shots line is too low. If they stay where they are, goal rates like his tend to cool.

There is noise around him too. On November 10, reports said he would welcome a fresh start elsewhere as a pending restricted free agent. ESPN reported the Flyers had received no formal trade request, and general manager Danny Brière called the report not correct. A trade would change his linemates, his power play unit and every number on this page, so check the roster before betting his props.
