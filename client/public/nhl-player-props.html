How ArakIce projects player props

A player prop is a bet on an individual's output rather than on who wins: shots on goal, points, goaltender saves. The edge comes from projecting that output more accurately than the sportsbook's line. ArakIce builds each projection from usage and role, the pace and quality of the matchup, rest and travel, and the goaltending involved, then compares it to the posted number. Where the two disagree, there is value; where they agree, there is not.

Props are fertile ground for edges because sportsbooks price hundreds of them every night and cannot sharpen each one. Use the search above to find any player, read the reasoning on their page, and open the app for tonight's live projection.
